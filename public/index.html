Flying

The jet is fly-by-wire: holding pull commands up to 10 G, limited by angle of attack. Hard turns bleed speed fast. You turn tightest and fastest at corner speed, about 320 kts at the start altitude and lower nearer the ground. The blue caret on the speed tape marks it. Faster than that you're G-limited and the circle gets wide, so use the airbrake (B) to slow down before a hard turn. Trade altitude for speed by diving, and use the afterburner to recover energy.

Pulling above 7.5 G fills the G-STRESS bar (about 8 s at full pull), and when it's full you black out briefly. Ease off to recover.

Guns

M61 cannon: 511 rounds at 100 per second, so you have about 5 seconds of fire. The barrels take a moment to spin up. Rounds slow down with drag and spread a little, so the gun is only effective inside about 1,000 m. Put the gun cross on the lead pipper (the circle with a dot). Hold Shift for fine corrections.

Sensors

You only know what your sensors tell you. Your radar (the fan at the bottom) sees ±60° off the nose out to about 40 km. Beyond 8 km you need it to find the bandit, and a radar track draws the box on the HUD. Tab locks it (STT). A lock gives your AMRAAM datalink updates, but the bandit's RWR warns them that you're locked on.

Radars filter out ground clutter, so a jet that is below you and flying across your line of sight (beaming, about 90° to you) disappears into the notch. Use it to break locks and defeat radar missiles. The RWR circle shows radars painting you (F) and active missiles (M). The missile warning only detects a burning rocket motor within about 5 km, so a Sidewinder that has burned out is silent.

Missiles

AIM-9 Sidewinder (Fox 2) is infrared. Press 1 and keep the bandit in the seeker circle until the tone goes steady. Lock range is longest from behind and when the bandit is in afterburner. The 9X can lock far off the nose with the helmet (padlock view T) or a radar lock, turns harder, and ignores most flares. The 9M needs the bandit near the nose and is easier to decoy.

AIM-120 AMRAAM (Fox 3) is active radar. Press 2; it needs a radar track. With a lock it receives datalink updates; without one it flies to where the bandit was. About 9 km from the target its own radar goes active ("pitbull") and it's on its own. The scale right of the altitude shows its launch zone: IN RNG means it can reach a target holding course, and SHOOT means no escape even if they turn and run.

Defending

Against IR: turn the missile onto your wing, pop flares (C) and come out of afterburner. Against radar: beam it and get below it so you sit in the notch, then drop chaff (Z). Chaff works best when you're beaming. Every missile runs out of energy, so turning hard or running away makes it fall short.
