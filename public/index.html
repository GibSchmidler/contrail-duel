Flying

The jet is fly-by-wire: holding pull commands up to 10 G, limited by angle of attack. Hard turns bleed speed fast. You turn tightest and fastest at corner speed, about 320 kts at the start altitude and lower nearer the ground. The blue caret on the speed tape marks it. Faster than that you're G-limited and the circle gets wide, so use the airbrake (B) to slow down before a hard turn. Trade altitude for speed by diving, and use the afterburner to recover energy.

Pulling above 7.5 G fills the G-STRESS bar (about 8 s at full pull), and when it's full you black out briefly. Ease off to recover.

Guns

Put the gun cross on the lead pipper (the circle with a dot) to hit. The arc around it shows range, and the cannon is effective inside about 1,200 m. Hold Shift for fine corrections.

Missiles

Keep the bandit inside the dashed seeker circle until the tone goes steady and you see LOCK. Lock range is much longer from behind (hot tailpipe) and when the bandit is in afterburner.

When MISSILE flashes, turn so the missile is off your wing (beam it), pop flares, and cut the afterburner. Missiles lose energy in hard turns.
